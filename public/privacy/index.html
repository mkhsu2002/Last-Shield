This policy explains how Last Shield, maintained by Reddit user u/OwnDifference7782, handles information in its current version.

Information stored on your device

Last Shield uses browser or app storage, when available, to remember your language preference, practice completion and daily best result. A saved result contains the challenge day, gameplay rules version, score, perfect clears, cleared meteors, best combo, and wave reached. The game remains playable if this storage is unavailable.

Reddit platform information

Reddit hosts the Reddit version of Last Shield and provides the account, community, post, and technical context needed to run apps. Last Shield uses the current post identifier to include a game link when you copy a result. When a moderator chooses to create a game post, the app uses Reddit’s API to create that post. Reddit handles platform authentication; Last Shield does not ask you for your Reddit password.

Scores and sharing

Community ranking is optional and available in the Reddit game. Starting a signed-in run creates a temporary session containing your Reddit account identifier, username, game post, challenge date, and start time, retained for up to two hours. If you choose to submit your score, the app sends the run’s gameplay inputs to its Reddit-hosted server to calculate the result. Gameplay inputs are processed for that request and are not retained as a replay. Your Reddit username and daily best score become public on the leaderboard for that subreddit. Scores and submission records, including account identifier, session identifier, gameplay totals, and submission time, expire no later than 30 days after the challenge day begins. You can remove your visible entry for a day from the leaderboard panel; submission records remain until their expiry to prevent duplicate processing. Practice and standalone web runs are not uploaded to the community board.

The score-and-strategy panel lets you write an optional note and copy the result to your clipboard, or select the text if clipboard access is unavailable. Notes remain in the current page unless you copy or share them. Opening the Reddit post does not send a comment: you choose whether to paste, edit, and submit it using Reddit’s controls. The game does not post or comment as you. If you share text yourself, the destination’s rules and privacy practices apply.

Optional Hall of Heroes and certificates

Joining the Hall is a separate, optional choice after a verified run. With your explicit agreement, the app keeps your Reddit account identifier privately and displays your Reddit username, optional callsign, best score, best combo and achievement dates long-term within that subreddit and rules season. These records do not expire with the 30-day daily board. Daily champion score summaries and tie counts may remain anonymously; a name is displayed only while its owner has an active Hall enrollment. Champion results are finalized after the UTC day ends and the two-hour submission window closes.

A verified single run exceeding 1,000,000 points qualifies for a Neo Babylon Citizen achievement certificate. The public certificate includes your username, optional callsign, score, best combo, date, season, issue time and public verification ID. Known AI demonstration runs are labeled and excluded from competitive rankings. The Hall does not retain gameplay input traces.

Use “Remove my Hall display & certificate” to withdraw the long-term public display and invalidate its certificate. A private withdrawal timestamp remains for up to 30 days to prevent old submissions from restoring the display; a new run and fresh agreement are required to rejoin. Anonymous daily score summaries may remain. This removal is separate from removing a daily leaderboard entry. Previously downloaded images or information you shared elsewhere cannot be recalled by the app. Sharing uses Reddit’s sharing controls or copied text. Public image export is a separate optional choice; nothing is posted automatically. Long-term storage lasts while the feature is operated, not a guarantee of perpetual service.

“Share certificate” uses Reddit’s sharing controls to share the game post, achievement text and public certificate ID. A recipient can open the game and check the certificate’s current validity. This does not automatically send a message or post on another service.

You can separately choose to create a public certificate image. This requires an additional agreement before sending the rendered PNG to Reddit’s media service. The app keeps the image URL, image hash and agreement time while your Hall record is active, to avoid duplicate uploads. Removing your Hall display clears that app-held image record and invalidates the certificate. Reddit-hosted images already exported, including an export in progress, may remain available: the app cannot recall them. You choose whether to open, save or share the public image. An image may be edited or copied; the current in-game certificate lookup remains the way to check its validity.

Other services

The current app does not add advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or payment processing. Reddit may process technical information and usage data under its own Privacy Policy: Reddit Privacy Policy

When you visit the standalone Last Shield website, its hosting infrastructure processes connection and request information needed to deliver the pages.

Removing information and contacting us

You can remove saved preferences and local scores by clearing the relevant browser or app site storage. In the Reddit game, use “Remove my entry for this day” to remove your visible daily ranking. Use the Hall removal control for its long-term display and certificate. For other app-held records, contact the maintainer; retention differs as described above. This does not delete Reddit posts, comments, or information held by Reddit; manage those through Reddit’s controls.
